Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to use GeetabyteERP?

No. It runs on your own computer and works without internet. You only need internet to send email, open WhatsApp or use the e-way bill API.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer, in a database installed with the app. A backup is saved every day automatically, and you can keep a second copy on a pen drive.

What happens if I do not renew the maintenance plan?

Your software keeps working for life. You just do not get new versions and support after the plan ends. You can renew later.

Can I move the license to a new computer?

Yes. Contact us with the new computer's Machine ID and we will issue a new key for it.

Can I start with Standard and move to Pro later?

Yes. Your data stays as it is. You only get a new license key for Pro.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. After installation you can use every feature for 30 days. Printed documents show a trial mark until you activate a license.
