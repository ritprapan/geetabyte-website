Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Free trial first

GeetabyteERP comes with a free 30-day trial, so you can check that it fits your business before you pay. Please use the trial before buying a license.

Software licenses

A license key is issued for one computer after payment. Once a license key has been issued, the license fee is not refundable. If the key does not work and we cannot fix the problem within 7 days of your complaint, we will refund the license fee in full.

Support plans (AMC)

You may cancel a yearly support plan within 7 days of payment for a full refund, if no support has been used. After that, the plan runs until the end of its term and is not refundable.

Odoo and custom services

Services are quoted in writing before work starts. If you cancel before work starts, any advance paid is refunded in full. If you cancel after work has started, we refund the advance minus the value of work already completed, as agreed in the quote.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you are charged twice or a payment fails but money is deducted, we will refund the extra amount after confirming it with our payment provider.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

How to ask for a refund or cancellation

Email info@geetabytesolutions.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 94285 87051 with your name, phone number and payment details.
