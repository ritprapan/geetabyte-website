Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Your business data

GeetabyteERP stores your business data (customers, products, invoices, payments and settings) in a database on your own computer. Geetabyte Solutions does not receive, upload or have access to this data.

Internet use by the app

The app connects to the internet only when you use a feature that needs it, such as sending email through your own email account or using an e-way bill service you configure. That data goes directly to the service you choose.

This website

This website does not use cookies or tracking. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which may record standard technical information such as your IP address.

When you contact us

If you fill the contact form, email or message us, we use your name, phone, email and message only to reply, share quotes and provide your license or support. Contact form details are stored in a private Google Sheet that only we can access. We do not sell or share your details with anyone.

Payments

Online payments are processed by our payment partner. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank login details.
