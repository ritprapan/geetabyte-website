Batch tracking

Every batch, in and out

Mark a product as tracked by lots once. Each goods receipt then asks for its batch number.

  • The oldest batch goes out first automatically, or choose the batch yourself
  • A batch cannot be sold beyond the quantity in stock
  • Returned goods go back into their batch
