Dashboards

One screen for every person in your business

Owner, sales team, accounts and store each get their own board. Pick a date range and compare with the previous period.

For the owner. Total sales vs last period, money to receive and pay, net cash flow, monthly target and a 12-month sales trend with next month's estimate.

For the sales team. Open quotations, orders waiting for invoice, CRM funnel, leads by source, win rate and quotations that expire this week.

For accounts. Overdue invoices, income vs expense for 12 months, receivable and payable ageing, bills to pay and GST payable this month.

For the store. Stock value, items running out, deliveries and receipts waiting, stock in vs out, fast and slow moving products.
